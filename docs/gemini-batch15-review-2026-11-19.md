## Method and limitations

Sources were screened by URL, DOI, normalized title, and selected translated-headline similarity. Source titles were checked on publisher pages; selected inaccessible pages and PDF reports were checked through independent access. A source title match does not verify every detail of a submitted summary. English renderings preserve original non-English headlines and require native-language review; submitted summaries require editorial review. Some Vietnamese and Chinese titles were rendered editorially when the automated translation service reached its limit. The Mekong Delta construction-project item `MSW-2302` uses a matching International Climate Initiative project page in place of an inaccessible submitted URL, which remains in `originalGeminiUrl`. When a source supported only a month or year, its precise date was left unverified. Contextual sources do not establish sand mining as the cause of reported changes. Coastal sand alternatives and basin-level tags do not identify individual inland extraction sites.
