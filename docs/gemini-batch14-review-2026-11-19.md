## Method and limitations

Canonical URLs, DOI values and normalized titles were compared against the prior archive and within the batch. Source titles were checked, with some initially inaccessible articles corroborated against publisher pages, Crossref or university repositories. A title match does not verify every claim in a submitted summary. Some scientific summaries were rewritten conservatively using accessible abstracts. English renderings of non-English titles retain the originals and require native-language review; submitted summaries also require editorial review. For sources with conflicting date metadata or only a month or year confirmed, the precise date was left unverified. The article `MSW-2075` concerns Tonle Sap turbidity: the accessible abstract says the impact of dam construction on turbidity was not significant, so the imported summary makes no causal attribution to dams. Contextual sources do not establish sand mining as the cause of reported changes. Coastal sand alternatives and basin-level tags do not identify individual inland extraction sites.
