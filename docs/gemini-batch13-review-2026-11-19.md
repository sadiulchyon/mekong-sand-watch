## Method and limitations

Canonical URLs, DOIs and normalized titles were checked against the prior archive and within the batch. Publisher-page titles were checked, with some PDFs and initially inaccessible pages independently checked through another access route. A title match does not verify every detail of the source or Gemini's submitted summary. Non-English titles have machine-assisted or editorial English renderings and retain originals; these renderings and submitted summaries need native-language and editorial review. Some Lao handoff entries actually link to English-language pages and are labeled English. When source metadata conflicted, the precise date was left unverified. The WWF consultancy terms of reference identifies a 2021–2023 work period but does not establish a specific publication day. Contextual sources do not establish sand mining as the cause of the described changes. Coastal sand alternatives and basin-level tags do not identify inland extraction sites.
