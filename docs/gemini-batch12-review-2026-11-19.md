## Method and limitations

Canonical URL, DOI, and normalized title were compared with the existing archive and within the batch. Source-page access and headline matches were checked, with selected blocked pages or PDFs reviewed separately. A headline match does not verify every detail of an article or every claim in Gemini's summary. Non-English headlines were machine-assisted and retain originals; translations and submitted summaries need editorial and native-language review. Where date metadata conflicted, the specific date was left unverified. Contextual studies and reports are not evidence that sand mining caused the changes described. Marine sand alternatives are tagged separately from inland extraction. A broad basin tag does not establish the location of an individual operation.

One report (`MSW-1693`) was confirmed to concern the Red and Da rivers and excluded. Document `MSW-1631` had a mismatch in the numerical range in its submitted summary, so the imported summary was reduced to what the abstract supports.
