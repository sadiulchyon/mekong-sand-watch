The 200 supplied records (Gemini IDs MSW-1426–MSW-1625) were compared against the full 1,099-record published archive and against one another by normalized URL, DOI and title, including original-language titles. The 125 accepted records retain their handoff IDs in `geminiOriginalId`; prior archive records were preserved unchanged.

Some publishers blocked automated access and some documents need closer metadata matching.

Most accepted records had their publisher headlines checked; submitted summaries generally need further editorial verification. Some official and scholarly sources were independently checked through publisher pages, bibliographic records or university-hosted manuscripts. A small number of inaccessible submitted links were replaced by accessible primary or author-associated sources, preserving the handoff URLs. English headline renderings preserve the originals and require native-language review. Conflicting publication dates are marked unverified; dates without publisher metadata are identified as handoff-reported. A coastal sediment survey is identified as a survey, not an extraction site. Contextual sediment and erosion studies do not establish sand mining as a cause of every reported change.

The complete submitted JSON is retained in the local project folder, including held and excluded records.
