# Mekong pilot editorial policy

This policy describes the new Mekong pilot. The Bangladesh project’s historical acceptance decisions, API keys and schedules do not carry over. On 14 November 2026 the owner requested a separate daily Mekong collector with automated source and editorial review, analogous to Bangladesh Sand Watch.

The initial four records are AI-prepared source summaries awaiting owner editorial review. Each links to an original source page that was read. The public display labels the dataset as a selected pilot. Inclusion does not independently validate allegations, estimates or legal interpretations.

Preserve named places and the actual geographic scope. Retain source publication dates separately from updates, observations, project periods and retrieval dates. Use `null` for an unverified publication date. Label every substantive estimate with its magnitude, unit, time basis, spatial extent and attribution. Stock is not a rate; sediment mass is not sand volume. Preserve conflicts rather than choosing a convenient figure.

Read original bodies before summarizing. Search excerpts, page clocks, inferred metadata and recommendation cards do not establish evidence. Mark abstract-only, OCR, blocked-body or incomplete reads explicitly. Display headlines in English, translating non-English source headlines faithfully; retain the original headline and identify the translation. Native-language review is needed for regional-language material. Exclude news substantially about gold mining, including disputes over whether an operation is mining gold or sand. Do not exclude sand stories merely because gold is mentioned in a price comparison.

Deduplicate canonical URLs and DOI. Check repeated reporting and overlapping projects before interpreting record counts as distinct events. A related institutional page and technical study may both be useful records without providing independent evidence of separate extraction events.

Registry membership is a collection plan. A selected page read is not a source-wide coverage audit. Zero records for a place do not establish zero extraction. Coverage counts must never be presented as mining intensity or extraction totals.

The daily collector searches in several regional languages and must read source bodies, verify date and figure evidence, and pass a separate model review before publishing. Its audit log records outcomes and source-body hashes. Native-language review remains necessary. Automated editorial acceptance means inclusion, not independent verification. The independent workflow uses only this repository’s secrets and deployment.
