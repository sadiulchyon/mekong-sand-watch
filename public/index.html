Transparent by design

Evidence you can trace.

An archive helps connect sources. It does not independently establish every claim they contain.

01 · Source first

Read the original.

Each record links to its source; the record identifies whether an article body or an abstract was read. Research findings, project descriptions, reporting, and official instruments remain distinct.

02 · Keep the context

Preserve place and time.

Named rivers, country scope, observation periods, publication dates, units, and uncertainty stay with each record. An unverified date remains unverified.

03 · Show the limits

Make gaps visible.

English summaries are subject to source and editorial review; original-language review remains necessary. Source limitations and conflicting claims are recorded. Multiple pages about one project do not become multiple extraction events.

Collection boundary

English is the initial interface language. Khmer, Lao, Thai, Vietnamese, Burmese, and Chinese sources need language-specific discovery and review before coverage can be claimed.
